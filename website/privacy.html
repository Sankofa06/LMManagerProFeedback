Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2026

Summary

LM Manager Pro is a local-first app. Your personas, scores, chat logs, and settings live entirely on your device in localStorage. We do not operate servers, collect analytics, or sell data.

What we store on your device

  • Persona roster, team configurations, episode definitions
  • Chat logs and scoring history
  • App settings (theme, prompts, machine URLs)
  • Preset prompts and timeline entries

All of this data lives in your browser’s localStorage. You can clear it at any time via your browser settings or the Wipe All Data button in the app.

What leaves your device

LM Studio servers (local)

When you connect to LM Studio, requests go to servers you control (typically localhost). No data is sent to us.

Cloud AI providers (optional)

If you configure a cloud provider endpoint, your prompts and chat messages are sent to that provider. We have no visibility into these requests. Relevant privacy policies:

  • OpenAI Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Alibaba Cloud Privacy Policy

DuckDuckGo (model lookup)

The app may open DuckDuckGo search links for model information. DuckDuckGo’s privacy policy applies to those requests.

Voice features

If you use voice input, your browser’s built-in Web Speech API processes audio locally or via your browser vendor’s servers, depending on your browser. We do not receive any audio data.

Analytics tracking and ads

None. We do not use analytics services, tracking pixels, or advertising networks.

Children’s privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your data, your device

You can export all your data at any time using the Export button in the app. You can delete all stored data by clicking Wipe All Data in Settings or clearing localStorage in your browser’s developer tools.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of any changes.
