Summary

LM Manager Pro is a local-first iOS and iPadOS app for managing LM Studio inference servers and cloud AI providers. There are no accounts, no analytics, no third-party crash reporting, and no background telemetry. Everything you create — machines, personas, chat history, episodes, scores — stays on your device unless you deliberately submit content through the public feedback form.

What we store on your device

  • App data (SwiftData): machine configurations, AI personas, teams, projects, chat sessions and messages, episodes, scoring history, and prompt templates. Stored locally in the iOS sandbox for this app only.
  • API keys (iOS Keychain): if you choose to use cloud providers, your API keys for OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and Qwen are stored securely in the iOS Keychain. They are used only to make API calls on your behalf and never sent anywhere else.
  • Settings (UserDefaults): non-sensitive preferences such as theme, default sort order, and feature toggles.

What leaves your device

The app makes network requests only in these situations:

  • Local LM Studio servers: the app connects to machines you explicitly add — over your local network or your Tailscale VPN — to load models and run inference. These connections never touch the public internet.
  • Cloud AI providers (only when you choose a cloud model): prompts, attached images, and any chat context are sent to the provider you selected. Each provider has its own privacy policy that governs that data:
    • OpenAI Privacy Policy
    • Anthropic Privacy Policy
    • Google Privacy Policy
    • Alibaba Cloud (Qwen / DashScope) Privacy Notice
    We do not relay, log, store, or proxy these requests — they go directly from your device to the provider's API endpoint.
  • Web search (DuckDuckGo, optional, per persona): if you enable web search for a persona, the app sends the persona's search query to DuckDuckGo.
  • In-app feedback (optional): when you deliberately submit feedback, the app sends the feedback type, title, details, app version, build number, platform, and iOS or iPadOS version to a developer-operated Cloudflare Worker. The Worker creates a persistent public issue in the LM Manager Pro Feedback repository. The form states that submissions become public and warns you not to include API keys, credentials, private hostnames or IP addresses, proprietary prompts, or other sensitive information.

The developer does not receive your prompts, responses, provider keys, chats, generated media, or local app database through the model, media, or search features.

Voice features

Speech-to-text (dictation) and text-to-speech use Apple's on-device speech and synthesis frameworks. Voice data does not leave your device for these features.

Analytics, tracking, and ads

None. The app contains no analytics SDKs, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers, no third-party trackers, and no cookies. The optional feedback relay is used only when you choose to submit feedback; it is not analytics or tracking.

App privacy disclosure

Feedback text is treated as Other User Content. The attached app, build, platform, and operating-system details are treated as Other Diagnostic Data. This data is used for app functionality and customer support, is not linked to an account or advertising profile, and is not used for tracking.

Children's privacy

LM Manager Pro is rated 13+ because it can connect to AI chat and content-generation services. The app does not knowingly collect information from children. Users should not submit personal or sensitive information through public feedback. User-configured cloud providers have their own age requirements and privacy policies.

Your data, your device

You can export your full dataset as JSON for backup or migration to a new device, and import it back with one tap. Deleting the app from your device removes all locally stored data, including Keychain entries created by the app.
